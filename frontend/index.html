📐 Association Mining Formulation

$$\text{Lift}(A \to B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(A) \cdot P(B)} = \frac{\text{Confidence}(A \to B)}{\text{Support}(B)}$$

A Lift > 1.0 indicates that item B is significantly more likely to be purchased when item A is present than by random chance alone.

🧠 Hybrid Scoring Formulation

$$S(i, j) = 0.55 \cdot \text{Norm}(\text{Lift}_{i \to j}) + 0.30 \cdot \text{Cosine}(\vec{v}_i, \vec{v}_j) + 0.15 \cdot \text{Sentiment}(j)$$

Guarantees high transactional co-purchase affinity while anchoring recommendations in culinary flavor harmony and verified user happiness.
